 Water Power Generator

1 Introduction
This project demonstrates a simple water-based energy generation model using a DC motor, battery and small fan.

2 Components Used
1. DC Motor
2. Battery
3. Small Fan
4. Connecting Wires
5. Water source

3 Working Principle
The flowing water is used to rotate the motor shaft through a suitable rotating mechanism. The DC motor can act as a small generator and produce electrical energy. The generated electricity can be used to demonstrate the working of a small fan, depending on the circuit arrangement.

4 Objective
To demonstrate the conversion of water energy into electrical energy using a simple model.

5 Applications
- Renewable energy demonstration
- Basic electricity generation experiments
- Educational science projects

6 Conclusion
This project helps us understand energy conversion and the importance of renewable energy sources.
